What we heard

The brief

Print-on-demand streetwear label. The wordmark is the identity: a lowercase engineered skeleton where the u and n share one spine, ending in a coral full stop that lands like a punch. Must survive garment tags, social avatars and a 16px favicon.

kineticengineeredminimalbold

Success: reads as crunchrr at a glance, distinct from streetwear clichés (bolts, flames, gothic), works one-colour and at 16px, seeds the storefront's design system.
